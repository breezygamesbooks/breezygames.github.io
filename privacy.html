Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Overview

We don't use analytics or advertising tracking tools on our website. However, our newsletter provider, Kit, may record whether subscribers open our emails or click links in them.

Newsletter

When you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address. We use it only to send you puzzles, solving tips, news about new books and their launch prices, and occasional requests for your ideas. We don't sell your email address or use it for purposes other than those described in this policy.

Our email service provider, Kit, stores your email address and processes it to manage subscriptions and send our emails. Kit may also record whether you open our emails or click links in them. To learn more, read Kit's Privacy Policy .

The signup form on our Free Puzzles page is provided by Kit. When you visit that page, your browser loads a script from Kit, and your email address is sent directly to Kit when you subscribe.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. If you have trouble, email us at breezygames.books@gmail.com and we will remove you from the newsletter.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. The GitHub Privacy Statement explains how. We don't have access to or control over those logs.

Cookies

Our own pages don't set cookies. The signup form is provided by Kit and may set its own cookies.

Links to other sites

We link to Amazon, which sells our books. If you follow a link to Amazon, its privacy policy applies to information collected through its website.

Your requests

You can ask us to delete your information by emailing breezygames.books@gmail.com, or through our contact page if you prefer.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
