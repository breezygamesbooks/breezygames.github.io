Understanding SE Ratings

What the numbers on our books mean

Every puzzle in our books is rated on the SE (Sudoku Explainer) scale, from 1.0, the easiest, to 11.9, the hardest.

The hardest technique sets the rating

A puzzle’s rating comes from the single hardest technique needed to solve it. Along the way you will also use easier ones, so a puzzle rated 3.2 still uses everything from the lower levels, and the X-Wing is the hardest move it asks for.

SE ratings measure logical difficulty, not solving time. Your experience and familiarity with the techniques will affect how challenging a puzzle feels.

Our books have friendly level names, from Beginner to Extreme, to help you find a place to start. Names like Easy or Hard can mean different things to different publishers, so the SE rating is what tells you the skill level, and every book states its own rating in the title or subtitle of its listing, wherever it’s sold. The SE bands don’t hold equal numbers of techniques, so we group our books by the techniques they need, and a book’s range may cross more than one band. See all the books.
