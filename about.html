Hello, we're James and Arnelyn

We're the two people behind BreezyGames. We both enjoy Sudoku, and we wanted to create puzzles that are enjoyable to solve and come with correct answer keys.

What started as a project for the two of us has grown into something we'd like to share with other Sudoku fans. We're glad you found us.

How it all began

It started with Arnelyn's love of Sudoku. Like many puzzle fans, she enjoyed working through a good puzzle. But every now and then, something would get in the way.

Some puzzles seemed to have more than one possible solution. Others came with answer keys that didn't match, or difficulty labels that didn't reflect the challenge very well. We wanted to do better. That's also why our difficulty levels are based on Sudoku Explainer (SE) ratings, which take the solving techniques into account.

James began creating puzzles for us to solve and check ourselves. It took time and plenty of testing, but eventually we had a collection we felt ready to share.

A little about what we do

We run BreezyGames together, although we each take care of different parts of the work.

James

James creates the Sudoku puzzles and books and looks after the website. Much of his time goes into building and checking puzzles, preparing answer keys, and making sure everything works as it should.

Arnelyn

Arnelyn looks after social media, advertising and the newsletter. She helps us keep in touch with Sudoku fans, share news and find out what readers would like to see next.

We're learning as we go

Creating Sudoku puzzles is just one part of running BreezyGames. There's also the website, newsletter, social media and advertising. We're learning how to manage all these things as we go.

We've still got plenty to learn, and we're sure there will be things we'll change and improve along the way.

BreezyGames is a small project run by the two of us. We want to keep making puzzles we're proud of, listen to feedback and make the experience better as we grow.

Thanks for being here

We hope you enjoy our puzzles. If you'd like to keep in touch, our newsletter is a good way to get a new Sudoku puzzle every week and hear what we're working on.

Happy solving!
James & Arnelyn
