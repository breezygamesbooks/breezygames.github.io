Hello, we're James and Arnelyn

We're the two people behind BreezyGames. We share a love of Sudoku and a simple wish: to make puzzles that people can enjoy and trust.

What started as a little project for the two of us has grown into something we'd love to share with other Sudoku fans. We're glad you found us.

How it all began

It began with Arnelyn's love of Sudoku. Like many puzzle lovers, she enjoyed the satisfaction of working through a good puzzle. But every now and then, something would spoil the experience.

Some puzzles seemed to have more than one possible solution. Others came with answer keys that didn't match, or difficulty labels that didn't quite reflect how challenging the puzzles really were. That's why every level we name comes with a Sudoku Explainer (SE) rating and the techniques it takes to solve.

We started looking for a better way. James began creating puzzles that we could enjoy without those frustrations. It took time, patience and plenty of checking, but gradually we built a collection we felt happy to share.

A little about what we do

BreezyGames is something we work on together. We each bring different things to the table, and that's part of what makes it enjoyable.

James

James creates the Sudoku books and puzzles and looks after the website. A lot of his time goes into building and checking puzzles, preparing answer keys, and making sure everything works as it should.

Arnelyn

Arnelyn looks after our social media, advertising and newsletter. She helps us stay connected with Sudoku fans, share what's happening and hear what people would like to see next.

We're learning as we go

We'll be honest: we're still fairly new to all of this. Creating puzzles is one thing, but running a website, looking after a newsletter, sharing things on social media and figuring out advertising are all part of the adventure too.

We're learning along the way, trying things, making improvements and occasionally discovering that there's more to learn than we first thought.

We're not a big publishing company or a team of experts. We're simply two people working together on something we care about. We want to keep making puzzles we're proud of and give other Sudoku lovers a pleasant experience along the way.

Thanks for being here

We hope you enjoy solving our puzzles as much as we've enjoyed creating them. And if you decide to join our little Sudoku community, we'd be delighted to have you.

Happy solving!
James & Arnelyn
