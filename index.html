Sudoku should be a pleasure, not a guessing game

There's something satisfying about settling down with a Sudoku puzzle. A pencil in hand, a few quiet moments, and the pleasure of watching a solution slowly come together.

That enjoyment can disappear when a puzzle has more than one solution, an answer key contains mistakes, or a difficulty label doesn't tell you what to expect. So we made BreezyGames the way we wished puzzles were made.

Every puzzle we create has exactly one solution and a matching answer key. We also make sure our puzzles don't repeat, so each one gives you something new to enjoy.

Difficulty that means something

A label like Easy or Expert only helps if it tells you something. Different puzzle makers use the same names for very different puzzles. So every level we name is tied to a Sudoku Explainer (SE) rating, which is based on the solving techniques a puzzle requires, not the number of empty squares or an estimated solving time. It's not the whole story of how hard a puzzle will feel to every solver, but it's a useful guide to what lies ahead.

Enjoy a break from the screen

Sudoku doesn't have to mean another app, another screen, or another notification.

There's a particular pleasure in solving on paper. You can take a puzzle to your favourite chair, work through it at your own pace, make notes in the margins, erase a mistake and carry on.

No battery, no charger and no distractions. Just you and the puzzle.
