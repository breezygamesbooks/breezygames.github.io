Difficulty levels you can understand

Not all Sudoku puzzles are equally challenging, and knowing what to expect makes choosing your next puzzle easier.

At BreezyGames, our difficulty levels are based on Sudoku Explainer (SE) ratings. These ratings reflect the solving techniques needed to complete a puzzle, rather than simply counting the empty squares or estimating how long it might take.

Of course, everyone solves puzzles differently, so a rating can't tell you exactly how difficult a puzzle will feel. But it gives you a useful idea of the challenge ahead.
