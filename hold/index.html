Sudoku should be a pleasure, not a guessing game

There's something satisfying about settling down with a Sudoku puzzle. A pencil in hand, a few quiet moments, and the pleasure of watching a solution slowly come together.

But that enjoyment can disappear when a puzzle has more than one solution, an answer key contains mistakes, or a difficulty label doesn't tell you what to expect.

That's what led us to create BreezyGames. We wanted puzzles we could trust, with a clear solution and an answer key that actually matched.

Every puzzle we create has exactly one solution and a matching answer key. We also make sure our puzzles don't repeat, so each one gives you something new to enjoy.

We're James and Arnelyn, and we're happy to share our love of Sudoku with you.

Difficulty that means something

Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert sound useful, but they don't always tell you much. Different puzzle makers can use those labels in very different ways.

We use the Sudoku Explainer (SE) rating system to give our puzzles a more meaningful measure of difficulty. Rather than relying on the number of empty squares or an estimated solving time, SE ratings are based on the solving techniques a puzzle requires.

It's not the whole story of how difficult a puzzle will feel to every solver, but it gives you a useful guide to what lies ahead.

Enjoy a break from the screen

Sudoku doesn't have to mean another app, another screen, or another notification.

There's a particular pleasure in solving on paper. You can take a puzzle to your favourite chair, work through it at your own pace, make notes in the margins, erase a mistake and carry on.

No battery, no charger and no distractions. Just you and the puzzle.
