Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

BreezyGames does not track visitors. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this site, and we don't ask for your name or any personal details beyond what is described below.

Newsletter

If you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address and nothing else. We use it only to send you puzzles, tips, polls, discounts and new book announcements. We don't use it for any other purpose, and we don't sell or share it with anyone.

Our email service provider, Kit, stores your email address and sends our emails on our behalf. Kit may also record whether you open our emails or click links in them. To learn more, read Kit's privacy policy at kit.com/privacy or visit their site.

The signup form on our Free Puzzles page is provided by Kit. When you visit that page, your browser loads a script from Kit, and your email address goes directly to Kit when you subscribe.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. If you have trouble, email us at breezygames.books@gmail.com and we will remove you from the newsletter.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. The GitHub Privacy Statement explains how. We don't have access to or control over those logs.

Cookies

Our own pages don't set cookies. The signup form is provided by Kit and may set its own.

Links to other sites

We link to Amazon, which sells our books. Amazon's privacy policy applies once you leave our site.

Your requests

You can ask us to delete your information at breezygames.books@gmail.com, or through our contact page if you prefer.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
